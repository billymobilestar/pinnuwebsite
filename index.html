About

A storyteller across every medium

Pinnu Sekhon is a multifaceted creative based in Vancouver, Canada. An artist at heart, she brings precision and emotional depth to every role she takes on.

Known for her work in Punjabi cinema alongside artists like Amrinder Gill and Hardeep Grewal, Pinnu continues to push boundaries across disciplines, telling stories that resonate with authenticity and cultural richness.
